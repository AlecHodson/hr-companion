Law Watch

Key employment law updates relevant to Complete Childcare Group managers.

SSP from Day 1 — in force April 2026

Statutory Sick Pay now applies from the first day of absence (no more 3-day waiting period). Ensure all absence records are updated accordingly.

Flexible Working — right from Day 1 (in force since June 2024)

All employees can request flexible working from their first day. Two requests per 12 months are permitted. Decisions must be made within 2 months.

Employment Rights Act 2025 — Unfair Dismissal from 6 months (Jan 2027)

From January 2027, employees will be protected from unfair dismissal from 6 months' service (currently 2 years). Review probation processes now.

Zero-Hours Contract Reforms — ERA 2025

Workers on zero-hours contracts will have the right to request a guaranteed-hours contract after 12 weeks. Review any bank/casual staff arrangements.

Fire & Rehire Restrictions — ERA 2025

Dismissal and re-engagement to change terms is now heavily restricted. Any variation to contracts must follow proper consultation processes.

Sexual Harassment — Preventive Duty (in force Oct 2024)

Employers must take reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment. Ensure the Contractor Code of Conduct is signed and staff training is current.

Carer's Leave — right from Day 1 (in force April 2024)

Employees can take up to 5 days unpaid carer's leave per year from day one of employment. No qualifying period required.

Neonatal Care Leave — in force April 2025

Parents of babies receiving neonatal care can take up to 12 weeks additional leave. Applies where baby receives neonatal care for 7+ consecutive days.
