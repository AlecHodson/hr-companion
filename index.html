Bradford Factor

Measures the impact of short-term absences. Formula: S² × D — where S = number of separate absence spells and D = total days absent in a rolling 52-week period.

Law Watch

Key employment law updates relevant to Complete Childcare Group managers.

SSP from Day 1 — in force April 2026

Statutory Sick Pay now applies from the first day of absence. Ensure all absence records are updated accordingly.

Flexible Working — right from Day 1 (June 2024)

All employees can request flexible working from day one. Two requests per 12 months. Decisions within 2 months.

Unfair Dismissal from 6 months — ERA 2025 (Jan 2027)

From January 2027, unfair dismissal protection begins at 6 months' service (currently 2 years). Review probation processes now.

Zero-Hours Contract Reforms — ERA 2025

Workers on zero-hours contracts will have the right to request guaranteed hours after 12 weeks. Review bank/casual staff arrangements.

Fire & Rehire Restrictions — ERA 2025

Dismissal and re-engagement to change terms is now heavily restricted. Any variation to contracts must follow proper consultation.

Sexual Harassment — Preventive Duty (Oct 2024)

Employers must take reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment. Ensure the Contractor Code of Conduct is signed and training is current.

Carer's Leave — right from Day 1 (April 2024)

Up to 5 days unpaid carer's leave per year from day one. No qualifying period required.

Neonatal Care Leave — in force April 2025

Parents of babies receiving neonatal care can take up to 12 weeks additional leave (7+ consecutive days of neonatal care).
